What surrogacy is

The short version, no legal language.

A gestational carrier

A woman carries a pregnancy for another couple. The embryo is usually created with the couple's eggs and sperm (or donor gametes), so the carrier isn't genetically related to the child.

Who it's for

Couples who can't carry a pregnancy themselves, same-sex couples, and single parents. Medical eligibility is decided by the clinic.

What it costs

Costs vary widely by clinic and city. Expect medical, legal, and compensation components. Ask the clinic for a full written breakdown before signing anything.

What the law says

India regulates surrogacy — this is the shape of it, not legal advice.

Surrogacy in India is governed by the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act, 2021. It permits only altruistic surrogacy for eligible couples, with conditions on age, marital status, and prior children. Rules and interpretations change — always confirm with the clinic and a lawyer.

Common questions

The things people actually ask first.

Is surrogacy legal in India?

Yes, under the 2021 Act, but only in the altruistic form and only for couples who meet the eligibility rules.

How long does the whole process take?

Realistically around 12 to 18 months from first consultation to birth, depending on matching and medical timelines.

Who is the legal mother?

Under the Act, the intended mother (or the single intended parent) is recognised as the legal parent once the process is completed properly.

Can NRIs do surrogacy in India?

Rules for overseas Indians have shifted over time. Confirm current eligibility with the clinic before planning.
